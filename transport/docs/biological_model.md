# Biological environmental fate module

`biology.reaction_tendencies(water_mass, bed_mass, water_volume_m3, bed_area_m2, parameters, environment)` operates in viable-count equivalents and seconds. It is coupled to advection, molecular diffusion and longitudinal dispersion by `transport.core`. It does not calculate infection risk or infer infectivity from organism detection.

The configurable terms are water/bed dark inactivation, optional calibrated Q10 temperature correction, depth-averaged solar inactivation, separately calibrated UV fluence response, particle-associated settling, first-order attachment/detachment, threshold-shear resuspension, and optional carrying-capacity-limited vegetative growth. Transfers between water and bed conserve viable inventory; inactivation removes inventory; growth is explicitly recorded as production. No spores or protozoan environmental cyst/oocyst stages grow by default. Germination and host life cycles are not represented.

Solar inactivation uses a supplied irradiance-matched coefficient times incident irradiance and depth-average attenuation `(1-exp(-k*z))/(k*z)` with a configurable shielded fraction. UV fluence and solar terms must represent independent exposures if both enabled: enabling both for the same light exposure would double count loss. Dark inactivation coefficients should exclude already represented sunlight effects. No universal death rate exists for these organisms across environmental conditions.

The preset catalog includes E. coli as a fecal indicator, Cryptosporidium oocysts, Giardia cysts, and a Bacillus subtilis spore surrogate. It records EPA references and explicit stage/assay limitations. Unsupported environmental coefficients remain null and processes disabled; missing coefficients for enabled processes raise errors. Editable metadata is returned by `parameter_definitions()` and defaults by `default_parameters()`.

Sources are recorded with sections and URLs in `transport/data/biological_presets.json`: [EPA UV Disinfection Guidance Manual](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-10/ultraviolet-disinfection-guidance-manual-2006.pdf), [EPA recreational water criteria](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-10/documents/rwqc2012.pdf), and [EPA Method 1623](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-08/documents/method_1623_2005.pdf). The regulatory UV treatment-credit table is retained only as reference metadata; it is never converted automatically into natural-sunlight kinetics. Method 1623 detection does not establish viability or infectivity. EPA sources do not supply Angkor-specific rates; calibration requires measurements and exposure spectra appropriate to the site.
