Scenario and limits

Source elevation, submerged bed geometry and vertical datum remain unverified. Fixed terrain; no erosion. The survey edge is a closed model boundary. No groundwater or observed regional inflows are included. This is transient redistribution of assumed initial water, not a steady or calibrated regional flow field.

Unit tracer is a numerical routing check, not a bacterial concentration or validated hotspot prediction. Each pond and moat component starts at its own terrain 25th percentile + 0.15 m. Canals outside storage use local cross-section terrain 25th percentile + 0.15 m, sampled every 10 m; high bank pixels remain dry. These are adjustable scenario assumptions; higher storage pixels may remain dry. No terrain is lowered to force connections.
